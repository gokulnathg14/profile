About

Thirteen years in the classroom, a specialization in data security, and a research programme built around trust systems.

Er. Gokulnath G is an Assistant Professor (Special) in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Saintgits College of Engineering (Autonomous), Kottayam. He holds a B.Tech and M.Tech, and is currently pursuing a Ph.D., with postgraduate specialization in Data Security.

Over a thirteen-year teaching career — alongside a year of non-teaching experience — his work has centred on Blockchain Technology and Cyber Security, spanning smart-contract verification, food-quality assurance on distributed ledgers, and privacy-preserving surveillance systems. He holds a copyright for the Rubrics Cube Encryption Algorithm and is an IUCEE-certified International Engineering Educator.

Beyond the classroom, he has organized and chaired hackathons and technical symposia — including four editions of Tink Her Hack and the Useless Projects 24-Hour Hackathon — and regularly serves as a resource person for AICTE- and institution-sponsored blockchain training programmes across Kerala and Tamil Nadu.
